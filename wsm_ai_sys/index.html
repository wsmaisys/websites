What makes WSM AI SYS different from standard web agencies?
Unlike traditional web agencies that build basic websites on generic WordPress or Wix templates with superficial ChatGPT wrappers, WSM AI SYS is a founder-led AI engineering studio operated directly by Wasim M Ansari (IIT Madras alumnus). We engineer bespoke 3D WebGL interfaces with living botanical tree dynamics and zero external CDN dependencies, and build complex multi-agent LangGraph workflows, Neo4j knowledge graphs, and custom MCP servers designed specifically for measurable commercial ROI.
What is Model Context Protocol (MCP) and why is it important?
Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that allows AI models like Claude and ChatGPT to securely execute tools and access enterprise data. WSM AI SYS develops authenticated MCP servers connecting AI models directly to internal databases, Neo4j knowledge graphs, and custom business APIs without exposing private data.
Why does WSM AI SYS enforce a Zero-CDN architecture?
A zero-CDN architecture is a deliberate performance and security-hardening engineering choice. External CDNs introduce single points of failure, latency spikes, and potential tracker snooping. By bundling all shaders, fonts, and scripts locally, WSM AI SYS ensures sub-second page loads, 100% uptime independence, and strict Content Security Policy (CSP) enforcement.
Do you work with businesses in Dehradun and across Uttarakhand?
Yes. WSM AI SYS is headquartered in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, in the foothills of the Himalayas. We work closely with regional hotels, clinics, manufacturing firms, and retail businesses, as well as serving high-growth startups and enterprises globally across India, North America, Europe, and Asia.
How does WSM AI SYS ensure AI systems do not hallucinate?
We implement Hybrid RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) combined with Neo4j Knowledge Graphs and BGE rerankers. By grounding AI responses in verified company documents and structured entity relationships with citation checks, we eliminate factual hallucinations and provide deterministic answers.
