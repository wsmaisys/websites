Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, machine-readable answers structured for search engines, LLM agents (Perplexity, SearchGPT, Claude, Gemini), and clients.

What makes WSM AI SYS different from standard web agencies?
Unlike traditional web agencies that build basic websites on generic WordPress or Wix templates with superficial ChatGPT wrappers, WSM AI SYS is an AI engineering studio led by an IIT Madras alumnus. We engineer bespoke 3D WebGL interfaces with living botanical tree dynamics and zero external CDN dependencies, and build complex multi-agent LangGraph workflows, Neo4j knowledge graphs, and custom MCP servers designed specifically for measurable commercial ROI.
What is Model Context Protocol (MCP) and why is it important?
Model Context Protocol (MCP) is the open standard introduced by Anthropic for connecting AI models to external tools, databases, and business services securely. WSM AI SYS builds authenticated, high-performance MCP servers that allow LLMs to safely query internal ERP systems, graph databases, and custom business logic without compromising security.
Why does WSM AI SYS enforce a Zero-CDN architecture?
External CDNs introduce single points of failure, latency spikes, and potential security or tracking vulnerabilities. By bundling all shaders, fonts, and scripts locally, WSM AI SYS ensures sub-second page loads, 100% uptime independence, complete GDPR compliance, and strict Content Security Policy (CSP) enforcement.
Do you work with businesses in Dehradun and across Uttarakhand?
Yes. WSM AI SYS is proud to be headquartered in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, in the foothills of the Himalayas. We work closely with regional hotels, educational institutions, manufacturing firms, and medical practices, as well as serving high-growth startups and enterprises globally across North America, Europe, and Asia.
How does WSM AI SYS ensure AI systems do not hallucinate?
We implement Hybrid RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) combined with Neo4j Knowledge Graphs. By grounding the AI in structured entity relationships and verified source documents with automated citation checks and BGE rerankers, we eliminate factual hallucinations and provide deterministic answers.
