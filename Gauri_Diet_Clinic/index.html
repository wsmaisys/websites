"My mission is to make evidence-based nutrition simple, personalised, and achievable — helping people build a healthier lifestyle that lasts."

I am Gauri More, founder of Gauri's Diet Clinic, dedicated to helping individuals transform their health through personalised nutrition, sustainable lifestyle changes, and practical, science-backed guidance. With over 8+ years of experience in clinical nutrition and diabetes care, I have had the privilege of guiding 6,000+ individuals across India and 12+ countries towards better health.

I hold a Master's degree in Food, Nutrition & Dietetics from Nirmala Niketan College of Home Science, University of Mumbai, and a Bachelor's degree in Botany from Mithibai College, Mumbai. My clinical experience spans renowned healthcare organisations, where I gained expertise in medical nutrition therapy, diabetes management, metabolic health, and patient counselling.

Nutrition is not just about following a diet — it's about creating habits that can be sustained for life. My expertise includes diabetes management, prediabetes reversal, weight management, PCOS, thyroid disorders, fertility nutrition, pregnancy nutrition, and digestive health.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can benefit from your nutrition programs? add

Our programs are designed for individuals looking to manage diabetes, pre-diabetes, PCOS, thyroid disorders, hormonal imbalance, weight management, and other lifestyle-related health concerns through personalised nutrition.

Are the diet plans personalised or generic? add

Every plan is customised based on your health goals, medical history, lifestyle, food preferences, culture, work schedule, and current eating habits.

Do I have to stop eating my favourite foods? add

No. We focus on sustainable changes by creating balanced plans that fit your lifestyle and include your preferred foods in a healthier way.

Do you provide Indian food-based diet plans? add

Yes. Our plans are Indian food-friendly and designed around traditional meals, regional preferences, and cultural eating habits. We also provide guidance for NRI clients with locally available food options.

Are consultations available online? add

Yes. We provide online consultations and support clients across India and globally through video calls and WhatsApp follow-ups.

How often will I have follow-ups? add

Follow-up frequency depends on your selected program. Regular reviews help us monitor progress and make necessary adjustments to your nutrition plan.

How long does it take to see results? add

Results vary from person to person depending on health condition, consistency, lifestyle, and adherence to the plan. Our focus is on sustainable, long-term improvements rather than quick fixes.

Do you provide exercise plans? add

We provide lifestyle guidance and recommend appropriate activity patterns. Exercise recommendations are personalised based on your goals and health status.

Can I continue the program if I travel or have a busy lifestyle? add

Yes. Our plans include practical solutions like travel-friendly options, flexible meal choices, and strategies that fit your routine.

Do you offer refunds if I change my mind? add

No. All program fees are non-refundable once the consultation process or program initiation has started. We invest time in assessment, planning, and personalised support for every client.

Is the BMI calculator on this site a medical diagnosis? add

No. The BMI calculator is a quick screening tool to give you a general sense of your weight category. It is not a medical diagnosis, and your consultation with Gauri will look at a more complete picture of your health.

How can I start my health journey? add

You can book a discovery call with our team to discuss your goals and choose the right nutrition program for your needs — reach out via the booking form, phone, or WhatsApp at +91 79725 21538.
