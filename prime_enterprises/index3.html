Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much can solar panels save on my electricity bill? +

Most homes and shops that switch to solar cut their electricity bill by 70 to 90 percent, since the panels generate the power you would otherwise buy from the grid.

What is the difference between on-grid, off-grid and hybrid systems? +

An on-grid system stays connected to the electricity board and sends extra power back to the grid. An off-grid system works on its own with batteries, useful where there is no grid connection. A hybrid system does both, giving grid backup plus battery storage.

How long does a solar installation take? +

A typical home or shop rooftop installation is completed within 3 to 7 days after the site visit and approval, depending on system size.

Do you provide solar pumps for farms? +

Yes. We supply and install solar water pumps for irrigation, so farmers can water their fields without depending on the electricity grid or diesel.

Where does Prime Enterprises operate? +

We are based in Satara, Maharashtra, and serve customers across major cities and towns in the state.
